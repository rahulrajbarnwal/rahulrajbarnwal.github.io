01 / About

Building high-performance Android experiences at scale.

With 9+ years as an Android Engineer, I'm passionate about crafting robust, user-centric Android applications using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose with MVVM Clean Architecture. Day to day that means designing and shipping scalable Android solutions that millions of users rely on, owning core modules for subscription, AI features and platform infrastructure, and leaning on agentic AI tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Gemini) to move faster without cutting corners on quality — while partnering closely with Product, Design, Business and Backend teams.
