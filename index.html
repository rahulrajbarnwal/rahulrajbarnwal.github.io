05 / Case Study

Sharing mobile foundations with Kotlin Multiplatform.

A production KMP initiative for the ET Newspaper app, focused on sharing core business logic and platform-independent infrastructure while keeping native platform experiences.

Engineering challenge

Maintain consistent business rules and data handling across mobile platforms without duplicating core implementation. The shared layer needed a clear boundary from platform-specific UI and integrations.

Implementation

  • Structured shared modules for business logic, models and networking.
  • Used Ktor for networking and Koin for dependency injection.
  • Used SQLDelight for shared persistence and Multiplatform Settings for key-value preferences.
  • Used Kotlin Coroutines and Flow for asynchronous work and reactive data streams.
  • Kept platform-specific concerns behind platform boundaries.
